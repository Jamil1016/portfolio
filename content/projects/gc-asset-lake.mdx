## The Problem

Asset-task data for a set of organizations had to be pulled from a third-party API,
validated, and made queryable — without re-pulling everything every run, and without
paying hot-database prices for cold historical data.

## Architecture

<MermaidDiagram>{`
graph LR
  API[(Swift API)] --> X[Incremental<br/>extract on last_updated]
  X --> V[Validate +<br/>normalize]
  V --> H[Hot tier:<br/>Supabase Postgres]
  V --> C[Cold tier:<br/>Parquet via DuckDB]
`}</MermaidDiagram>

Extraction is incremental — only records whose `last_updated` changed are pulled. Data is
validated and written to two tiers: a hot tier (Supabase Postgres) for live queries and a
cold tier (Parquet, queried with DuckDB) for cheap historical storage.

## Stack

Python · DuckDB · Parquet · Supabase Postgres · asyncpg · pytest

## Highlights

- 2.6M+ rows validated through the pipeline.
- Incremental sync keyed on `last_updated` — no full re-pulls.
- Hybrid hot/cold storage: live queries on Postgres, cheap history on Parquet.
